## Sample Compliance Clauses Document

Clause 1 - Transaction Limitations:

Cross-border transfers between Country A and Country B must not exceed $10,000 per calendar

month.

Clause 2 - Prohibited Transactions:

Transactions involving sanctioned entities from Country X are strictly prohibited.

Clause 3 - Reporting Requirements:

All transactions over $5,000 must be reported within 24 hours.

Clause 4 - Transaction Limitations:

Currency conversion for single transactions must not exceed $15,000 without prior approval.

Clause 5 - Prohibited Transactions:

Transfers to unverified third-party accounts are strictly prohibited.

Clause 6 - Reporting Requirements:

Suspicious activity must be logged and reported to the compliance team within 12 hours.

Clause 7 - Transaction Limitations:

ATM withdrawals are limited to $1,000 per day per account.

Clause 8 - Prohibited Transactions:

Use of shell companies for fund transfers is prohibited.

Clause 9 - Reporting Requirements:

All large cash transactions above $10,000 must be documented and submitted weekly.

Clause 10 - Transaction Limitations:

Wire transfers must not exceed $100,000 without compliance officer authorization.

Clause 11 - Prohibited Transactions:

Payments to politically exposed persons without enhanced due diligence are prohibited.

Clause 12 - Reporting Requirements:

Every transaction involving more than one jurisdiction must be reviewed and reported.

Clause 13 - Transaction Limitations:

Clause 14 - Prohibited Transactions:

Sending funds to accounts flagged by international watchlists is prohibited.

Clause 15 - Reporting Requirements:

Weekly summaries of all international transfers must be submitted to the finance director.

Clause 16 - Transaction Limitations:

Investment transactions exceeding $200,000 require executive approval.

Clause 17 - Prohibited Transactions:

Use of anonymizing VPNs during transaction initiation is prohibited.

Clause 18 - Reporting Requirements:

High-frequency accounts must be flagged and reported monthly.

Clause 19 - Transaction Limitations:

Online foreign exchange transfers must not exceed $2,000 per transaction.

Clause 20 - Prohibited Transactions:

Payments involving cryptocurrency mixers are strictly prohibited.
